Drop your hoodie photos in this folder.

Name them like this and they appear on the site automatically:
  <slug>-1.jpg   main photo (e.g. night-market-1.jpg)
  <slug>-2.jpg   shows on hover and in the product gallery
  <slug>-3.jpg   more gallery photos
  lookbook-1.jpg ... lookbook-5.jpg   the photo strip on the home page

Slugs are listed in src/data/store.js. Recommended size: 1200 x 1500 px (4:5), JPG.

Home page extras:
  story-1.jpg, story-2.jpg, story-3.jpg   the "how one hoodie was made" example (sketch, test piece, finished)
  collection-street.jpg, collection-minimal.jpg, collection-retro.jpg, collection-outdoors.jpg   collection tiles
